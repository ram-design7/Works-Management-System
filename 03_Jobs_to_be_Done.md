# Works Management System — Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) Analysis

## About the Jobs-to-be-Done Framework

The **Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)** lens looks past *what a stakeholder does in the system* to *why they show up at all*: what progress are they trying to make in their work life, and what would make them "hire" this system to make that progress happen. For each persona (see `02_Personas.md`), this document sets out:

- **Primary Job-to-be-Done (JTBD statement)** — in the standard *"When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome]"* format.
- **Functional Job** — the practical task they need done.
- **Emotional Job** — the feeling they're chasing or trying to avoid.
- **Social Job** — how they want to be seen by others as a result.
- **Desired Gains (To-Be)** — what the new system should deliver to satisfy this job.
- **Where the job plays out** — the workflow stages where this job is "hired."
- **Success looks like** — a measurable signal that the job has been done well.

## Job 1: Priya — the Centre-Level Proposal Originator

**JTBD statement:** *When my centre needs new or upgraded sports infrastructure, I want to raise a clear, well-justified proposal and know exactly where it stands at every stage, so I can secure funding for my athletes without chasing files or guessing at its status.*

**Functional Job:** Capture and submit a complete, well-justified infrastructure demand (category, scope, cost, linked asset) and track it through to approval.

**Emotional Job:** Feel confident that her request will be seen and fairly considered — relief from the anxiety of "did HQ even receive this?" or "did it get lost in someone's inbox?"

**Social Job:** Be seen by her Centre Director and by athletes/coaches as someone who gets facilities built, not someone who "sends emails into a void."

**Desired Gains (To-Be):**
- A guided proposal form with standardized categories/work types so nothing is missed.
- A personal dashboard showing every proposal she's raised and its live status (Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Returned).
- Ability to save drafts and resume later, and to see exactly what needs fixing when a proposal is returned.
- Direct linkage to the existing asset record when proposing an upgrade, so her justification is automatically grounded in facts.

**Where the job plays out:** Stage 1 (Proposal Creation) primarily; re-enters at Stage 2 if the proposal is returned for revision.

**Success looks like:** She can tell an athlete or coach exactly which stage their facility request is at, on demand, without making a single phone call to Delhi.

## Job 2: Rakesh — the HQ Engineering Division Desk Officer (Reviewer/Estimator)

**JTBD statement:** *When dozens of centre-wise demands arrive every year, I want to consolidate, prioritise, and route them accurately against the available budget, so I can put a defensible, well-justified list in front of the Director General without last-minute scrambling in Excel.*

**Functional Job:** Aggregate proposals centre-wise, cross-check them against budget, coordinate multi-division review, and keep the route-assignment and estimate-tracking data accurate and current.

**Emotional Job:** Escape the dread of reconciling multiple disconnected Excel sheets right before a DG meeting; feel in control of a process that today depends on manual diligence and memory.

**Social Job:** Be viewed by the DG and Operations Division as the reliable single source of truth for "where every work stands" — not someone who says "let me check and get back to you."

**Desired Gains (To-Be):**
- Proposals arrive pre-structured and centre-tagged, ready for joint review without manual re-entry.
- Approved work details carry forward automatically into route assignment, estimation, and tendering — no duplicate data entry.
- One consolidated, filterable view of every work's current stage, agency, and estimate status.
- A defensible, timestamped record of every review comment and prioritisation decision.

**Where the job plays out:** Stages 2 through 6 most intensively (Review & In-Principle Approval, Route Decision, Estimation/Sanction, Tendering, Contract Award).

**Success looks like:** The prioritised, centre-wise list for DG approval is generated from live system data in minutes, not compiled overnight before the meeting.

## Job 3: The Director General (DG) — the Competent Authority

**JTBD statement:** *When I have to approve and steer a nationwide portfolio of infrastructure works with a fixed annual budget, I want a consolidated, trustworthy view of what's proposed, what's approved, and what's at risk, so I can make fast, well-informed decisions and be confident they'll be executed as directed.*

**Functional Job:** Review prioritised proposals, approve/reject at each authority gate (in-principle, route, administrative, technical), and monitor portfolio-wide execution health.

**Emotional Job:** Confidence that approvals are being acted on faithfully — freedom from worrying that a decision made on file gets lost or misapplied downstream.

**Social Job:** Be seen — by the Ministry, by SAI leadership, and by centres — as decisively and transparently steering national sports infrastructure investment, backed by evidence rather than anecdote.

**Desired Gains (To-Be):**
- A single portfolio dashboard with drill-down from national summary to individual work.
- Every approval (in-principle, route, administrative, technical sanction) captured against the work record itself, not just in a file noting.
- Early visibility into at-risk works (delayed tenders, overdue milestones, expiring DLPs) rather than discovering issues late.

**Where the job plays out:** Stage 2 (In-Principle Approval), Stage 3 (Route Decision), Stage 4 (Administrative Approval/Technical Sanction) as direct decision gates; ongoing portfolio-level oversight across all 12 stages.

**Success looks like:** Any work's full approval history and current status can be pulled up in seconds during a review meeting, with no need to request files in advance.

## Job 4: The Engineering In-charge — the Site-Level Executor

**JTBD statement:** *When a contractor is executing a work at my site, I want to record progress, verify claims, and flag quality or schedule issues as they happen, so I can protect SAI's interests and ensure the facility is delivered to specification without disputes later.*

**Functional Job:** Supervise execution against the sanctioned scope, verify measurements and claims against site reality, conduct and document site visits, coordinate testing, and certify completion.

**Emotional Job:** Confidence that his sign-off is defensible — that if a dispute arises months later, there's a clear, contemporaneous record proving what happened and when, rather than relying on memory or paper notes that can go missing.

**Social Job:** Be trusted by both HQ and the contractor as a fair, diligent verifier — not someone who rubber-stamps claims or, conversely, causes needless delay through unclear objections.

**Desired Gains (To-Be):**
- Contract Agreement, PBG, and LOC details available digitally against the work for quick reference during execution.
- Geo-tagged photos and structured progress templates (done/pending) for every site visit — an unambiguous, timestamped record.
- Digital capture of Measurement Book entries, claims, and quality test results tied directly to the BOQ, reducing disputes.
- DLP and warranty deadlines tracked with automatic alerts, so post-handover obligations aren't missed.

**Where the job plays out:** Central to Stage 7 (Execution & Field Monitoring), Stage 8 (Measurement, Claim & Payment), Stage 9 (Quality Assurance & Testing), Stage 10 (Project Closure), and Stage 11 (Post-Closure/DLP).

**Success looks like:** Every claim he certifies is backed by geo-tagged, timestamped evidence that would hold up if questioned in an audit — with zero reliance on a physical register that could be misplaced.

## Job 5: The Executing Agency Representative — CPWD Officer / PSU Project Manager / Private Contractor

**JTBD statement:** *When I've completed a measurable portion of sanctioned work, I want to submit and get my claim verified and paid quickly and transparently, so I can maintain cash flow and avoid disputes over what was actually delivered.*

**Functional Job:** Execute work per the sanctioned BOQ and contract, submit progressive claims with supporting measurements, and receive milestone-based payments.

**Emotional Job:** Reduce the frustration and financial stress of delayed or disputed payments; trust that verification is based on transparent, objective criteria rather than opaque manual sign-off.

**Social Job:** Be seen by SAI as a reliable delivery partner worth awarding future tenders to — reputation directly affects future business.

**Desired Gains (To-Be):**
- Digitally tracked BOQ-linked measurements and claims, giving a transparent, shared basis for what's approved and why.
- Milestone and payment status visible in the system rather than requiring follow-up calls.
- Contract terms, penalty clauses, and DLP/warranty obligations available digitally, reducing ambiguity and disputes.

**Where the job plays out:** Stage 5 (Tendering, as bidder), Stage 6 (Contract Award), Stage 7 (Execution), Stage 8 (Measurement, Claim & Payment), Stage 9 (Quality Testing — for third-party audit testing), Stage 11 (DLP obligations).

**Success looks like:** Claims are verified and paid within a predictable, known timeframe, with clear visibility into any deductions and why they were applied.

## Job 6: The Headquarters Portfolio Monitor — RC/HQ Oversight Officer

**JTBD statement:** *When I'm responsible for the health of many concurrent works across centres, I want a live, consolidated view of physical and financial progress against plan, so I can spot slippage early and escalate before it becomes a crisis rather than after.*

**Functional Job:** Track physical vs. planned progress and financial progress (funds released) across all works in scope; compile and circulate periodic reports; escalate at-risk works.

**Emotional Job:** Move from reactive firefighting (finding out about a delay only when a report is finally compiled) to proactive control.

**Social Job:** Be seen by senior leadership as someone who catches problems early, not someone who is caught off guard in a review meeting.

**Desired Gains (To-Be):**
- Live dashboards generated directly from system data, at both RC and HQ levels, with drill-down into any individual work.
- Standardised reporting templates auto-populated from execution and payment data — no more manual compilation.
- Early-warning visibility into schedule slippage, financial progress mismatches, and upcoming DLP/warranty expiries.

**Where the job plays out:** Continuous oversight across Stages 7–12, with particular emphasis on Stage 7 (Execution & Field Monitoring).

**Success looks like:** At-risk works are identified from the dashboard days or weeks before they would have surfaced in a manually compiled report.
